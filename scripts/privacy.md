## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, and authentication credentials when you register
- **Profile Information:** Business type, team size, professional role, and inbox management goals collected during onboarding
- **Phone Number:** Your mobile phone number for SMS delivery, verified via one-time passcode
- **Payment Information:** Billing details processed and stored by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full credit card numbers
- **Communications:** Messages you send to our support team

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, briefings triggered, and interaction timestamps
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
- **Log Data:** Server logs including access times, error logs, and API request records
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** Session cookies for authentication; local storage for preference persistence (such as disclaimer acceptance)

### 2.3 Email Content (Transient Processing)

When you connect your email account, we access your email messages **transiently** for the sole purpose of generating briefings:

- Email content is read, classified, and summarized by our AI system
- The full text of emails is **never permanently stored**
- Only a brief encrypted summary and metadata (subject line, sender category, timestamp) are retained in our logs
- Email content is wiped from our processing systems immediately after summarization

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **AES-256-GCM Encryption:** All OAuth tokens and email snippets are encrypted before storage
- **TLS/HTTPS:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit
- **Helmet Security Headers:** HTTP security headers including Content Security Policy, HSTS, and X-Frame-Options
- **Rate Limiting:** Automated rate limiting to prevent brute-force attacks
- **Audit Logging:** Sensitive actions (briefing triggers, subscription changes, phone verification) are logged for security review
- **Access Controls:** Employee access to user data is strictly limited on a need-to-know basis

Despite these measures, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights and freedoms, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service:

- **Account data:** Retained for the duration of your account plus 90 days after deletion
- **Briefing logs:** Retained for 12 months, then automatically purged

- **Audit logs:** Retained for 24 months

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support@inboxbrief.com.

## 8. Email Account Access

When you connect a Gmail or Outlook account:

- We request **read-only** access to your email messages
- We do **not** request permission to send, delete, or modify your emails
- OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored in our database
- You can revoke our access at any time from your email provider's security settings or from your InboxBrief account settings
- Revoking access immediately stops all email processing
